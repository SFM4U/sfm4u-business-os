# How the Owner thinks and works — and how SFM4U was built (history page)

Owner: MD SHALA UDDIN (founder of SFM4U, Brooklyn NY and Noakhali, Bangladesh). Written by Claude from the Owner's own words on 2026-11-22. Add to it, never delete.

## How the Owner thinks
- **Whole system first.** He thinks in complete systems: from buying stock to the customer's door. He never wants a single feature in isolation; every piece must fit "one software".
- **Build once, use everywhere, sell it too.** What SFM4U builds for its own businesses must also become a product others can buy, as a full software or as separate modules, at a fair low price.
- **Own everything.** Our own code, theme and builder (Noakhali Builder), our own marketplace. Outside software only where unavoidable (WordPress, WooCommerce). His reasons: security, because his AI runs on these sites, and independence.
- **One place, no duplicates.** One master copy of each software, one place to find it, and nothing lost. "If it is all there, we never lose anything."
- **Brand matters.** The SFM4U logo, colours and look must be exact everywhere. He checks sizes and proportions himself (for example, the logo's bracket arrows must keep the logo's own size).
- **Plans for generations.** The company, the numbering system and the records must survive him: history pages, skills and clear records so the next generation and the next AI can continue.

## How the Owner works with AI
- He speaks Bangla by voice, often long messages. Understand the intent from the whole message; ask at most one short question.
- He does not code. He wants the AI to do the work end to end, then show the result (screenshots, live link), short and in Bangla.
- He talks mainly with Claude. Claude records everything (Factory, skills, Master Notes) and gives other AIs (ChatGPT/Codex, Hermes) their work. When ChatGPT is slow, Claude builds it itself.
- He gives broad permission. He still expects safe steps: test first, keep everything (switch off instead of delete), and clearly tell him the one thing only he can do (a password, a GitHub key, an approval click).
- He watches the cost: the weekly token limit matters. Work efficiently, deploy only real releases, keep replies short.
- He wants every plan and decision written into skills, so he never has to explain twice.
- His corrections are final. When he says "this is not what I meant", redo it his way (for example: the Dashboard must open OUR module pages, not Dokan pages).

## History — 21 to 22 November 2026 (what was built and why)
1. **Business Software grows.**
   - Wholesale, retail price audiences, purchase cost, the one-page product sheet (A–Z), member cards, POS, receipts, ERP link and delivery were built into one plugin. "SFM4U Business OS" was renamed "SFM4U Business Software".
   - Versions 0.9.10 → 0.9.23 in two days.
2. **Dashboard and modules.**
   - The Owner asked for one dashboard where every part is visible; then he corrected it: everything must be our own module.
   - Result: a main Dashboard, a dashboard for each module, a module contract, a Module Manager (add/update/roll back, SFM4U modules only) and the SFM4U design kit.
3. **The plan to sell like Dokan, but better.**
   - A catalog of ~130 modules (platform, shops, products, prices, orders, payments, delivery, POS, stock, customers, marketing, reports, apps), plus future products: Noakhali Builder, theme, delivery platform, ride share, booking.
4. **marketplace.sfm4u.com born** (22 Nov 2026).
   - A new site on the same hosting.
   - Our own theme with the sfm4u.com header, menu and footer and the exact logo wordmark.
   - Our own store plugin, with 144 items: complete and upcoming modules, business plans, all SFM4U software.
   - A protected release store: one ZIP per version, direct access denied.
5. **First add-on modules built by Claude and live on algibazar.com:** E07 Order Tracking and E04 COD Control (fake-order guard).
6. **The Owner's rule for releases:** one ZIP per release on marketplace. Our sites install and get updates like Elementor/Dokan. Customers buy and download the same file. Every site that runs a module shows that it is live.
7. **The Owner's Mac:** Claude Code CLI and the Hostinger plugin installed, and the Hostinger connection added for ChatGPT, so either AI can work if one subscription ends.
